The Gathering

Since the turn of the millennium, Branziert has convened every December — a fine group of fellows who make time for what matters. This is the 27th gathering. Each year, a different location. Always the same spirit: no pretension, proper food, sharp conversation, and something worth talking about come January.

This year takes the group to the Fife coast. Pratis House: a Scottish manor with 180° views across the Firth of Forth, three nights, and a weekend shaped entirely by us. Below is everything you need — from the moment you land at Edinburgh airport to the last coffee on Sunday morning.

The Venue

An 18th-century Scottish manor, entirely ours.

Built as part of the Lundin & Montrave estate in the late 1700s, Pratis House was fully renovated in 2022 — heritage bones, 21st-century everything else. The whole property is taken on exclusive use for Branziert, sleeping 14 across 7 individually named bedrooms.

Perched above the Fife countryside with sweeping 180° views across the Firth of Forth from Edinburgh to St Abbs Head. Flexible, high-quality countryside accommodation offering complete privacy and the quality hospitality that defines the Pratis name.

Travel

Getting to
Pratis House

01
By Car — from anywhere in the UK
Most of the group will be driving. Head for the Forth Road Bridge (A90) into Fife, then east on the A92 towards Kirkcaldy and Leven. Follow the B927 to Pratis House. Ample on-site parking.

Sat-nav: KY8 5NX. The final mile is unlit rural track — take your time after dark.
From Glasgow ≈ 1h 20min · Manchester ≈ 3h 30min · London ≈ 6h
02
Flying into Edinburgh (EDI)
A handful of the group will be flying in. From Edinburgh Airport, hire a car at the terminal and follow the A90 north over the Forth Road Bridge into Fife, then the A92 east towards Leven. Sat-nav to KY8 5NX. Around 50 minutes from the airport.
≈ 50 min from EDI
03
Flying but no hire car? Tap up the group
If you're flying in without a car, check with the group — someone driving up will almost certainly be passing Edinburgh or happy to divert. The WhatsApp group is your friend. Failing that, Edinburgh Tram to Waverley, ScotRail to Kirkcaldy or Markinch, then a pre-booked local taxi for the final leg.
Sort it in the group chat
04
Train to Kirkcaldy or Markinch
If you're coming by rail, ScotRail runs regular services to both Kirkcaldy and Markinch from Edinburgh Waverley and most central Scotland stations. From either station, pre-book a local taxi for the final 15–20 minutes to Pratis — rural cabs don't materialise on demand.
Pre-book your taxi

In December, Fife is dark by 4pm and the B927 is unlit. Plan your route before you leave the motorway. There's a well-stocked BP/M&S garage on the A915 en route from Edinburgh — worth a stop if you need provisions. Better to arrive settled and stocked than late and empty-handed.

What To Do

Activities &
Local Highlights

Old Course — St Andrews The Home of Golf · 20 min

The ballot for public tee times runs daily — enter online before 14:00 the day prior. No guarantee, so always have a backup plan. The New Course and Jubilee Course offer strong alternatives with easier availability in December.

standrews.com ↗︎
Leven Links Classic Scottish Links · 3 min

Branziert's back garden. One of the oldest links courses in Scotland with golf dating to the mid-1800s — thought to be the first course in the world with 18 tees and 18 separate greens. A six-time Open Championship final qualifying venue. Book online direct, no ballot faff.

leven-links.com ↗︎
Wild Scottish Sauna Wood-fired · Sea plunge · Kingsbarns & Elie

Wood-fired mobile saunas across five locations on the Fife and Angus coast: West Sands St Andrews, Kingsbarns Beach, Shell Bay Elie, Eden Springs, and Forbes of Kingennie. Private sessions for up to 12. Book well ahead — the best slots fill fast. Check live availability on their site.

wildscottishsauna.com ↗︎
Surf & Beach Adventures Blown Away · West Sands, St Andrews

Surf lessons on West Sands Beach, St Andrews — £43 per adult, wetsuits and all equipment provided. Blown Away also run land yachting, kayaking, paddleboarding and SUP tankers. Brilliant for a group morning. Pre-booking essential; contact them directly for group rates and December availability.

blownaway.co.uk/surfing ↗︎
Kingsbarns Distillery Single Malt Scotch · 18 min

A beautifully converted farm steading on the Cambo Estate producing an accessible, fruit-forward single malt. Guided tours and tastings available — the Dream to Dram story is worth hearing. Exceptional gift shop for those who need a bottle for the road.

kingsbarnsdistillery.com ↗︎
Fife Coastal Run Running Route · from the doorstep

The Fife Coastal Path doubles as one of Scotland's great running routes — sections between Elie, Leven, Lower Largo and St Andrews are dramatic at any pace, and brutal in the best way in a December headwind. Blow away the previous evening on the North Sea coast. No booking required.

fifecoastalpath.co.uk ↗︎
